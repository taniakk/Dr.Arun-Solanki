About Dr. Arun D. Solanki

Expertise, Experience & Excellence in Gastroenterology

Dr. Arun D. Solanki is a highly qualified Gastroenterologist with extensive training in Internal Medicine and Medical Gastroenterology. His academic journey includes MBBS from Seth G. S. Medical College, MD in General Medicine from Government Medical College, Nagpur, and DM in Medical Gastroenterology from Madras Medical College, Chennai.

Professional Journey

Following his postgraduate training in General Medicine, Dr. Solanki served as Senior Resident at TNMC & Nair Hospital, Mumbai. He later completed his super-specialty training in Medical Gastroenterology, developing expertise in diagnosing and managing disorders of the digestive system, liver, pancreas, and gastrointestinal tract.

Since 2022, he has been practicing as a Consultant Gastroenterologist and is associated with multiple healthcare institutions, providing comprehensive and patient-focused care using advanced diagnostic and therapeutic techniques.

Academic & Research Contributions

Dr. Solanki has actively contributed to gastroenterology research and has presented scientific work at national forums, including ISGCON and TNISG conferences and meetings.

His published research includes studies on iron profile in sickle cell disease, portal biliopathy, gastrointestinal bleeding, and high-resolution manometry in patients with dysphagia, reflecting his commitment to evidence-based clinical practice and continuous academic growth.

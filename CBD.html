Understanding CBD Stones & Pancreatitis

CBD (Common Bile Duct) stones occur when gallstones move into the bile duct and block the flow of bile. This may lead to severe abdominal pain, jaundice, infection, and pancreatitis.

Pancreatitis is inflammation of the pancreas and may be acute or chronic. Early diagnosis and timely treatment are essential to prevent serious complications.

Common Symptoms

Severe Abdominal Pain
Nausea & Vomiting
Jaundice
Fever & Infection

ERCP Expertise

ERCP (Endoscopic Retrograde Cholangiopancreatography) is a specialized procedure used to diagnose and treat disorders of the bile ducts and pancreas. It enables removal of CBD stones, stent placement, and management of pancreatic disorders without major surgery.

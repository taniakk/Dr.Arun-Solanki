Irritable Bowel Syndrome (IBS)

Comprehensive evaluation and personalized treatment for Irritable Bowel Syndrome (IBS) to help manage symptoms and improve quality of life.

What is IBS?

Irritable Bowel Syndrome (IBS) is a common digestive disorder that affects the large intestine. It can cause abdominal discomfort, bloating, constipation, diarrhea, or a combination of both.

Although IBS does not permanently damage the intestines, its symptoms can significantly affect daily activities. Proper diagnosis and personalized treatment can provide effective symptom control.

Common Symptoms

Abdominal Pain
Bloating & Gas
Diarrhea
Constipation

Common Triggers

Stress & Anxiety
Spicy Foods
Dairy Products
Irregular Eating Habits

Diagnosis & Evaluation

Clinical Assessment

Detailed review of symptoms, diet, lifestyle and medical history.

Laboratory Tests

Blood and stool investigations to rule out other digestive conditions.

Endoscopic Evaluation

Endoscopy or colonoscopy may be advised when necessary.

Personalized Treatment Approach

Dietary Guidance

Customized meal plans to reduce bloating, discomfort and bowel irregularities.

Medication Management

Targeted medications for pain relief, constipation and diarrhea control.

Lifestyle Modification

Stress management, exercise and healthy routines for long-term symptom control.
